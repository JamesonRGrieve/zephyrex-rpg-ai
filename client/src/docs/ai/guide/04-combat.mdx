# Combat

A fight starts when the story starts one: the fight check reads the line, and the VTT starts the encounter
and rolls initiative. From then the VTT's order runs the round: your turn, then each AI-run combatant's.

## Who fights whom

Sides come from each character's disposition, never two fixed teams. Everyone in the fight has their own
stance (hostile, neutral or friendly) and who they are hostile to, read from the wiki's attitudes and the
story so far. Someone with no quarrel stays neutral; three-way fights and turncoats work; a surrender or a
betrayal changes a stance mid-fight. Where a stance is unknown, the decision model chooses among the VTT's
options.

## Your turn

Say what your character does. The decision model matches your line to the actions open to you; the VTT rolls
it, the result is a roll line, and the narrator tells what happened, never changing the result. A line that
names no action open to you is answered with a reminder of what you can do.
