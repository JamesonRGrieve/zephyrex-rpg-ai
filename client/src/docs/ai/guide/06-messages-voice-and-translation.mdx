# Messages, voice and translation

## A message's menu

- **Edit**: the edited line becomes a new alternative beside the old one, and play runs on from it.
- **Regenerate**: another telling of a reply. On your own line it resubmits the line as a new alternative,
  and the round runs again from it. The old branch stays, a page of the branch arrows away.
- **X-card**: on any message, to step away from what it holds, with a reason for the GM.
- **Feedback**: a note on a message for manual review.

## Voice

Every message can be read aloud: one message, or from that message to the end. Each character has their own
voice. Voice can prepare clips ahead or play them as they come; Stop ends playback.

## Translation

Translation is a setting for the whole app: replies are shown in your language, and the story is kept in the
campaign's.
