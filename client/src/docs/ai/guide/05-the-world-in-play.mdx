# The world in play

## Names are links

Every name of a person, place or item of the campaign in the story is a link. Tap it to open its wiki page in
the drawer. From a character's seat the page shows only what a player may see: the player-visible entry and
**what your character knows**. The GM sees everything, and who knows what.

## Learned facts

When a scene reveals someone or something to a character, it is recorded as something that character knows
of. That opens the record to them in the drawer. The GM can add or remove facts from the record's page.

## The map

The map is the VTT's scene, with the party's tokens and the people present. Scope out to see the town instead
of the house: each building with people in it shows one group token, and tapping it lists who is there.
