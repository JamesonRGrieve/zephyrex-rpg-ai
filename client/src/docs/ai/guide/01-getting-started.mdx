# Getting started

Zephyrex RPG AI is where a campaign is played with AI voices: an AI game master, an AI party, and the people
of the world, all speaking in character. It is the top of the Zephyrex RPG suite:

- **the wiki** holds the world: settings, campaigns, scenarios, people, places, items and what they know;
- **the VTT** holds the table: the map, tokens, dice, character sheets and the fight;
- **the AI** (this app) gives them voices, and reads and writes the other two through their APIs.

The AI never invents mechanics. Rolls, legal actions and changes of state go through the VTT; a decision
model only ever chooses among options the VTT or the wiki supplies.

## Your seat

When you start a scenario you choose a seat.

- **The GM.** You run the world; the party is AI. Your lines are the narration.
- **A character.** You play one character; the AI plays the rest of the party, the people you meet, and the GM.

From a character's seat you see what that character may see. A person's page in the drawer shows the
player-visible entry and the facts your character has learned, never the GM's file.

## Starting a campaign

1. Open **Campaigns** and pick a scenario.
2. Choose your seat, and who plays in the party.
3. As the scenario's GM, set the **starting level**: the pregens are built up to it, as high as the rules
   pack allows (20 for the SRD).
4. Press **Start the campaign**. The scenario is copied into your own playthrough, the party is placed on the
   starting scene, and the opening is told.
